About

I'm Jones Anderson, a student in the Bay Area. I got hooked on making when my brother got a 3D printer for Christmas, and I've gotten my hands on eight more since.

A friend told me about DEF CON, which got me into badges. My first one, SAO MANY SAOs, holds 25 add-ons at once; I sold it at DEF CON 33. Now I design PCBs, build printers, machine parts on CNC mills and lathes, and make LED helmets for Halloween.

I'm also on FRC team 5940 BREAD, where I work on fabrication and scouting data.
